Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Rajkumar S

Rajkumar is an engineer by graduation, with a master’s in business management. For more than 15 years he has worked on portfolio management services, mutual funds, and in finance and auditing firms — which means he has seen the same family’s money from three different sides: how it is invested, how it is sold, and how it actually reads on paper at the end of the year.

His work is simple to describe. Understand what the client needs. Read what the market is doing. Then set a goal that is realistic — an amount and a year that can actually be reached, not a number that only looks good in a presentation.
