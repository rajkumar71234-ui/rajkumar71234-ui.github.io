Solutions

Each category has exactly one job.

We do not rank these. A solution is only good relative to a goal, a time horizon and the money you already have working elsewhere. Here is what sits in each, in plain language.

01

Fixed Income

Bonds

A fixed rate for a fixed period, from the government, a PSU or a company. You know on day one what you get and when.

Debt Fund

The same idea, pooled and managed daily, and you can exit in a day. Where money you need in one to three years sits.

02

Equity

Mutual Fund

A professional manager and a spread of companies, from a few hundred rupees a month. The simplest honest way to own equity.

PMS

A concentrated portfolio held in your own demat, run by a professional manager. SEBI minimum ₹50 lakh.

AIF

A pooled fund with a wider mandate than a mutual fund — long-short, structured, sector-specific. SEBI minimum ₹1 crore.

Direct Equity

Shares held in your own name and chosen by you. More control, far more concentration — a small, deliberate slice.

Foreign Equity

Ownership outside one country and one currency. Worth adding once the domestic core is already doing its job.

03

Private Equity

Cat 2 AIF

Category II funds investing in unlisted and private companies. Capital is called over years and returned over years, not on demand.

Unlisted Direct Equity

Shares in a company that has not listed yet, bought directly. Priced by negotiation, sold only when a buyer appears.

04

US NRI Corner

Non-PFIC AIF

An India fund structured to sit outside the PFIC rules, so a US filer is not pushed into punitive tax treatment on ordinary growth.

GIFT City Fund

Invested through India’s international financial centre. Dollar denominated, and simpler to hold and repatriate from abroad.

05

Commodity

Physical Commodity

Gold and silver you can hold — coins, bars, jewellery. Easiest to own, but it carries locker cost, making charges and a purity question.

Gold & Silver ETFs

The same metal held electronically, priced to the market and sold in a day. No locker, no making charge, no argument about purity.

06

Insurance

Life

Pure term cover, sized to your income, your loans and the years your dependants still need. Cheap when bought early.

Vehicle

Own-damage and third-party cover for the car or two-wheeler, with the add-ons that actually get claimed and none that do not.

07

Tax Consulting

Tax Planning

The year’s investments and salary structure decided well before March, so the saving is a by-product of a sound decision.

Filing & Capital Gains

Returns filed correctly, with gains from funds, shares and property computed, set off and carried forward properly.
