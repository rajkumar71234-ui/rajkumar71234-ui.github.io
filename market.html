The chart is drawn from our own month-end record of the BSE 500 — index level and trailing twelve-month earnings per index point, kept since 1999. No data vendor publishes an earnings series for an index, so this is our own work rather than a licensed feed. The dotted section carries the last actual earnings figure forward to the end of the financial year at the rate earnings have grown over the past twelve months; it is an extrapolation, not a forecast, and nothing should be read into it. The index is the BSE’s rather than the Nifty 500 because NSE does not licence its prices for embedding; the two track each other closely. The combined market value and combined profit below come separately from the current 500 constituents’ own filings; the earnings per index point and the multiple beside them come from our own series, so the panel and the chart never disagree.
