Market

What the market is doing, and what it means for your goal.

Markets move every day. Most of that movement does not change what you should do. This page is where we set out what we are watching and what we are deliberately ignoring.

What we watch

A short list, checked often.

Where the cycle sits, what money is costing, and what is already priced in.

An elephant does not move for every sound in the forest. It moves when there is a reason. We read the market the same way — the point is not to react to the day, it is to know whether anything has changed for your goal.
