P/E is the whole index’s market value divided by its companies’ combined trailing profit; EPS is the index level divided by that P/E. The 500 names are today’s constituents, so earlier years describe the companies in the index now, not the index as it stood then. The chart is the BSE’s index rather than the Nifty 500 because NSE does not licence its prices for embedding; the two track each other closely.
