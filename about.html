Why us

Wealth management is a service, not product selling.

Most of the industry sells products. We do not see it that way. Wealth management is not product selling — it is a service.

Every person has a different aspiration and a different goal. So an adviser should listen more, instead of talking about products. We simply understand where you are standing today, analyse whether the goal is practical or not, and park the money only there.

That is why we call ourselves minimalists. We understand what is actually needed, so the energy is not wasted and the plan stays clear.

Our mark

Why the elephant is our mark.

The elephant is an endurance animal, and it moves very slowly. Here, slow or fast is not what is important — whether it moves in the right direction is what is important.

By the way, an elephant can sense up to 20 km. In a day it can walk 20 km on average, and up to 200 km in the extreme, to reach the destination without losing energy.

What we do
  • Ask about your loans and your insurance before your investments.
  • Put the goal, the amount and the year in writing.
  • Tell you the cost of every product in rupees, not percentages.
  • Review against the gap to the goal, and reallocate when your life changes.
  • Tell you which categories you do not need.
  • Say “I do not know”, and come back with the answer.
What we do not
  • Recommend a product before we understand your position.
  • Sell insurance as an investment.
  • Use a market fall or a market high as a reason to transact.
  • Move you between schemes to create activity.
  • Promise a return, a rank, or a sure multibagger.
  • Leave you holding anything you cannot explain yourself.
