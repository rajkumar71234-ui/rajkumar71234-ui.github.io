About us

Less, but focused — applied to money.

Minimalism is not about owning little. It is about owning only what has a job to do, and knowing what that job is.

A wardrobe works this way. So does a house. Money is no different. Most portfolios in India are not too small — they are too crowded. Eleven policies. Six funds holding the same forty companies. A deposit sitting against a goal that is twenty years away. Nothing there is wrong on its own. Together it becomes noise, and noise hides the only question that matters: will you actually get there.

So we subtract. One goal, one clear reason, one place for the money to sit. What is left is short enough to hold in your head — and a plan you can hold in your head is a plan you will actually keep.

Why us

Advice is a service, not a product.

Most of the industry sells you something and then goes quiet. Managing assets is not a sale — it is a service, and a service has to stay active.

Active means three things, in this order. We understand you: your income, your expenses, your loans, who depends on you and how much fall you can watch without losing sleep. We establish where you stand today, honestly, with everything on one page. And then we work out how the goal is actually reached — the amount, the year, and the route — and we stay with it, reviewing against the gap and reallocating when your life changes, not when a new scheme is launched.

That is professional work, and it exists for one reason: money is emotional. Fear sells at the bottom, greed buys at the top, and the family that has no one to call does both. A professional does not remove the emotion — nobody can. He puts a written plan between the emotion and the decision, gives you a straight answer when the market is ugly, and keeps you doing the boring thing long enough for it to work.

What we do
  • Ask about your loans and your insurance before your investments.
  • Put the goal, the amount and the year in writing.
  • Tell you the cost of every product in rupees, not percentages.
  • Review against the gap to the goal, and reallocate when your life changes.
  • Tell you which categories you do not need.
  • Say “I do not know”, and come back with the answer.
What we do not
  • Recommend a product before we understand your position.
  • Sell insurance as an investment.
  • Use a market fall or a market high as a reason to transact.
  • Move you between schemes to create activity.
  • Promise a return, a rank, or a sure multibagger.
  • Leave you holding anything you cannot explain yourself.
